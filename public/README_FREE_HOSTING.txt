## How to Publish for 100% Free on GitHub Pages (Zero Billing, No Credit Card):

1. Go to https://github.com and log in (or create a free account).
2. Click **'New'** to create a new repository. Name it: `sarvagya-ai` and set it to **Public**.
3. Click **'uploading an existing file'** and drag & drop the `index.html` file from this folder.
4. Click **'Commit changes'**.
5. In your GitHub repository, click **Settings** (top tab) ➔ **Pages** (left menu).
6. Under 'Build and deployment', choose **Branch: main** and folder **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
7. Wait 30 seconds: Your app is PUBLISHED to the whole world at:
   https://<YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME>.github.io/sarvagya-ai/

## How to Run Offline (No Internet Server):
Simply double-click `index.html` on any Windows, Mac, Linux, or Android device. It opens directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari with full voice recognition, dual cameras, stopwatch, and calendars!
